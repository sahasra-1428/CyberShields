Daily Defense Tip

The Golden Rule of UPI Transfers

You NEVER need to enter your UPI PIN or scan a QR code to receive money. A UPI PIN is ONLY entered to deduct money from your account. If anyone asks you to enter your PIN to "accept" a refund or payment, it is an outright scam.

Action: Immediately decline the request and report on 1930 Tip 1 of 18

The 10 Commandments of Citizen Cyber Hygiene

1. The Receiving Myth: Entering a PIN always sends money, never receives.
2. The Video Call Test: Indian police & CBI never interrogate on video calls.
3. The Slash Check: Verify the host right before the first single slash / in a URL.
4. Golden Hour 1930: Report financial scams within 2 hours to freeze bank conduit nodes.
5. Zero APK Trust: Never install .apk packages received via WhatsApp, Telegram, or SMS.
6. Remote Access Ban: Never install AnyDesk, TeamViewer, or RustDesk on a caller's request.
7. AI Voice Passphrase: Agree on a secret family word to counter AI deepfake voice emergency calls.
8. SIM Swap Warning: If mobile network drops to 'No Service' abruptly, call your telco immediately.
9. Auth App Over SMS: Switch banking and email 2FA to Google Authenticator / physical keys.
10. ATM Wiggle Test: Pull and wiggle the ATM card insert bezel before inserting any debit card.
