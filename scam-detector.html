    How to Identify Scam Messages & Social Engineering

    Behavioral Engine Guide

    1. Anatomy of a Scam Message

    URGENT: Electricity Office: Your power will be disconnected tonight at 9:30 PM due to unpaid bill. Immediately update details or call officer at 9876543210 or visit http://power-bill-pay.xyz
    Urgency Trigger Forces victim into panic to bypass critical reasoning.
    Authority Claim Impersonates government bodies, banks, or utility services.
    Tight Deadline "Tonight at 9:30 PM" prevents calling official helplines.
    Personal Phone Number Directs you to the fraudster instead of an official landline.
    Phishing Action Link Harvests net banking passwords or triggers remote APK installs.

    2. Critical Social Engineering Myths

    👮 Myth 1: "Police or CBI can arrest you over a Skype/WhatsApp video call."

    Reality: "Digital Arrest" does not exist in Indian or international law. Law enforcement agencies (CBI, ED, Police, Customs) never conduct interrogations, trials, or arrests via video calls, nor do they demand money transfers to "verify funds."

    🏛️ Myth 2: "The message says 'Government / RBI Approved', so it must be real."

    Reality: Scammers deliberately print the names of trusted authorities (RBI, Police, Income Tax) precisely to stop victims from questioning the message. The claim itself is evidence of an assertion, never proof.

    4. The 6 Heuristic Pillars Inspected by CyberShield

    1. Artificial Urgency & Fear

    Triggers: "immediately", "within 24 hours", "account blocked", "fine of ₹50,000".

    Defense: True organizations send formal notices, not panic SMS messages.
    2. False Financial Lures

    Triggers: "won ₹25,00,000", "lottery claim", "earn ₹5,000/day liking videos".

    Defense: You cannot win a lottery or prize you never entered.
    3. Authority Impersonation

    Faking police, CBI, RBI, tax officials, or bank security teams.

    Defense: Verify through official portals; never reply to unsolicited contacts.
    4. Credential & OTP Harvesting

    Demanding PINs, OTP codes, card CVVs, or Net Banking passwords.

    Defense: Bank staff will NEVER ask for your OTP or password.
    5. Isolation & Secrecy Demands

    Commands like "stay on call", "do not tell family", "room lock required".

    Defense: Isolation is the hallmark of coercion scams. Disconnect immediately.
    6. Embedded Phishing Links

    Directing victims to fake portals or prompting APK downloads.

    Defense: CyberShield automatically extracts and deep-scans all embedded links.

    4. Four-Step Citizen Defense Checklist

    1. Take a 5-minute pause: High-pressure urgency is designed to make you act before thinking. Step back.
    2. Never use the numbers or links in the message: Call the official customer helpline printed on the back of your debit card or bill.
    3. Remember the golden rule of OTP: An OTP is required ONLY to debit money from your account, never to receive money.
    4. Report immediately: If you receive an impersonation attempt, report it on CyberShield or the National Cyber Crime Portal (1930).
